Privacy Policy

App: Word of Day (com.english.widget.eng_widget_app)
Developer: IdeaRoost
Last updated: October 29, 2026

Summary

Word of Day is a fully offline English vocabulary app developed by IdeaRoost. We do not collect, store on our servers, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no internet permission at all: it physically cannot send any information anywhere. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party data sharing.

Data stored on your device only

The app keeps the following data locally on your device, in the app's private storage. It never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app:

  • Favorite words (local file favorites.json);
  • Your own example sentences (local file user_examples.json);
  • Photo associations you attach to words (app-private folder);
  • App settings such as interface language (local file settings.json);
  • Home screen widget content (system shared preferences).

Permissions

  • Notifications — used only for the optional daily "word of the day" reminder and the quiz reminder. The app asks for this permission once, only after you tap "Get started" or rely on the reminder, and works fine without it.
  • Camera / photos — used only when you choose to add a photo association to a word, via the standard system picker. The selected photo is saved in the app's private storage on your device only.

Children

Word of Day is a general-audience language learning app and is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect any data from any user, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new "Last updated" date. Given that the app has no internet access and collects nothing, material changes are not expected.
